## Audio

### Freesound recordings

The horn, the lock chirp, the chimes and the pedestrian warning (`Assets/sounds`) are recordings that
their authors released as CC0 (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They were cut and converted to PCM for this mod. The
pedestrian warning ships in the mod but is switched off in this version.

| Sound | File | Author | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horn | `horn.pcm` | Julien_Matthey | https://freesound.org/s/436589/ |
| Lock chirp | `chirp.pcm` | Filmscore | https://freesound.org/s/830110/ |
| Chime | `chime_on.pcm`, `chime_off.pcm` | greatsoundstube | https://freesound.org/s/629312/ |
| Pedestrian warning | `pws.pcm` | itinerantmonk108 | https://freesound.org/s/745146/ |
