# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added
- Jeremy sells the Yesla Model 3 for $60,000: electric hum, 0-50 km/h in 3.4 s, any paint colour, saved with
  the game, every co-op player can own one.
- Assisted Driving: set a destination in the Yesla phone app, press `Y` in the driver seat. Steer, brake or
  press `Y` again to take over.
- The car takes the fastest route around police checkpoints, brakes early for bends, signals, and stops at red
  lights and crossings.
- Autopark at the destination: a free spot or the kerb, backed in or nose in (`ParkDirection`).
- Drive profiles Chill, Standard and Mad Max (`DriveProfile`); Mad Max ignores speed limits and red lights.

### Phone app
- Map with search, your route and Send to car. Go to target sends the empty car there to park.
- Actual Super Summon brings the car to you and follows you while you walk.
- Honk and flash, Lock, Sentry Mode, Frunk and Trunk lids, paint colour and licence plate. Owner only.
- Your car in its own paint, turned the way your camera looks at it.

### In the car
- First-person view on `C`: a detailed cabin, the visualisation on the centre screen.
- Visualisation on `N` (panel, full screen, off): lanes, route, cars, people, buildings, crossings, parking
  spots, traffic lights.
- Storage at the nose and the rear, one inventory. Steering wheel, wipers, bonnet and boot move.
- Automatic headlights at night, on for a minute after a night drive. Tail lamps, turn signals, the reverse
  light lights the ground.
- Recorded horn, lock chirp, and chimes when Assisted Driving takes over and hands back.
