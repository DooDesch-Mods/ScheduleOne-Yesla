# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-16

### Added
- Jeremy sells the Yesla Model 3 for $60,000: electric hum, any paint colour, saved with the game, every co-op
  player can own one.
- Assisted Driving: set a destination in the Yesla phone app, press `Y` in the driver seat. Steer, brake or
  press `Y` again to take over.
- The car keeps to speed limits, stops at red lights, waits for traffic and backs out when it gets stuck.
- Driving visualisation on `N` (panel, full screen, off): lanes, blue route, cars, people, traffic lights,
  speed limit and the next turn.
- Phone app remote: Actual Super Summon, honk and flash, Lock and Sentry Mode, for the owner only. First-person
  view on `C` shows the visualisation on the centre screen.
